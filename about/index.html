About Me

Welcome to my homepage! I am a PhD candidate in Data Science at Queensland University of Technology (QUT), under supervision of Dr. Mahdi Abolghasemi, Prof. Raja Jurdak and A/Prof. Sara Kahlifa. I am a principal member of Forecasting Insight and Decision-making Group. I am also a member of the Energy Transition Centre and the Centre for Data Science in QUT. Previously, I obtained a Master's degree in Data Science at The University of Queensland (UQ), 2025, and a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at Nanjing Tech University (NJTech), China, 2022.

My research interests revolve around hierarchical forecasting, federated learning, and deep learning, especially their application in the real world. My experience involves topics across domains, such as Time Series Forecasting by ML/DL, and Optimisation in supply chain planning. I am also passionate about using theories from mathematics, statistics, and data science combined with domain knowledge to solve practical problems across industries.

Please feel free to drop me an email. I am looking forward to any possible discussion and collaboration in research and industry.
